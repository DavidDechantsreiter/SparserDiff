ANALYSIS:
  - Ego graphs are LARGE (50-399 nodes)
  - Both algorithms use per-graph randperm/rejection, so speed is comparable
  - Novel uses O(k) memory (k = edges to sample) vs O(max_condensed) for Original
  - Quality: Baseline scores better on most graph metrics at 100 epochs
    (100 epochs is early-stage for Ego; full training uses 100k epochs)
  - Spectral MMD: Novel wins (lower is better)
  - NumNodes W1:  Novel wins (lower is better)
  - KEY WIN: Novel uses significantly less memory on large graphs
