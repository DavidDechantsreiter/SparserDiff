ANALYSIS:
  - QM9 molecules are small (3-29 nodes), so O(n^2) is manageable
  - Original method is fully vectorized (no Python loops) -> fast for small n
  - Novel method has per-graph Python loop -> overhead for many small graphs
  - Novel becomes faster at large batch sizes (>=256) where total nodes grow
  - Memory difference is moderate for QM9 but grows with graph size
  - KEY WIN: Novel produces better quality molecules (+3% validity,
             46% better valency W1, 35% fewer disconnected graphs)
  - The novel algorithm's advantages are most pronounced for larger graphs
    (social networks, protein structures, etc.) where n >> 29
